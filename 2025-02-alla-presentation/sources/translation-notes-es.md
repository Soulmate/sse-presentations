## Decisions to confirm

1. **"Europa" → "Unión Europea"** (Aduanas slide, list of countries). The English deck was recently changed from "Europe" to "European Union", so the Spanish now matches it. The docx table still says "Europa".
2. **"Contacts" → "Contacto"** (last slide). This follows the docx table. The Spanish pptx had left this heading in English.
3. **Bold phrases on the NFO / Charter / Transporte terrestre slide.** The English deck highlights key phrases in bold, and the Spanish pptx had none. The Spanish now bolds the matching phrases:
   - envíos más voluminosos y pesados
   - paquetes grandes o sobredimensionados
   - envíos de gran volumen o con requisitos especiales
   - aeronave exclusiva
   - carga sobredimensionada
   - transporte terrestre rápido y fiable
   - vehículos dedicados y conductores profesionales
